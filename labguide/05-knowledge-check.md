## 🧹 Clean Up (Optional)

Your Azure resources are deleted automatically when the lab ends. The Fabric items you created live in your workspace, so you can delete them yourself:

1. In Microsoft Fabric, open the **<inject key="Fabric Workspace Name" enableCopy="false"/>** workspace.
1. Select **Workspace settings**.
1. In the **General** tab, scroll down to **Delete workspace**, and then select **Remove this workspace**.
1. In the warning dialog, select **Delete**. A deleted workspace can't be recovered.

> **Note:** Only clean up after you've run every validation in this lab. Validations check the items in your workspace.
